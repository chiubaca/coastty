## Question

Which 4–6 no-key public Stations provide the requested mix of lofi hip-hop and chill or melodic techno, permit this app to link to and play their streams with any required attribution, use formats supported by OpenTUI, and remain reliable enough for a curated default set? Record candidates, evidence, tested endpoints, formats, metadata behavior, terms, attribution requirements, and operational risks in a linked research asset.

## Comments

### Resolution - 2026-08-19

Research asset: [Lawful and Dependable Stations](../research/lawful-dependable-stations.md).

Only Nightwave Plaza passed both the technical and explicit-permission gates, conditionally for a free, ad-free app that identifies the Station and displays current artist/title attribution. It is vaporwave/future-funk rather than exact lofi hip-hop or melodic techno. The technically suitable exact-genre candidates either prohibit third-party app playback or provide no affirmative permission, so the required lawful 4-6 Station set is not verified. The resulting product decision continues in [Decide How to Resolve the Station Sourcing Gap](05-decide-station-sourcing-gap.md).
