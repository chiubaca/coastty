## Question

Given that only the chill-adjacent Nightwave Plaza explicitly permits this free, ad-free third-party player while exact lofi and melodic-techno candidates lack permission or prohibit app playback, which lawful sourcing strategy should the destination adopt: narrow or broaden the Station/genre target, pursue written provider permissions before implementation, or replace direct third-party radio streams with another no-key source model? Resolve the destination impact through a live grilling session before Station-dependent implementation is specified.

## Comments

### Resolution - 2026-08-19

Ship this effort with Nightwave Plaza as its sole Station rather than blocking on uncertain provider outreach or treating public endpoints as permission. Present Nightwave Plaza honestly as vaporwave/future funk, display its required current artist/title attribution, and omit Station-selection controls from the one-Station UI. Preserve the Selected Station abstraction and runtime extensibility so additional lawfully sourced Stations can be added later.

Additional Station sourcing, written provider permissions, and exact lofi hip-hop or melodic-techno coverage move beyond this destination into a later effort. This keeps the current player lawful, verifiable, and reachable without weakening the evidence standard established by [Choose Lawful and Dependable Stations](01-choose-lawful-dependable-stations.md).
