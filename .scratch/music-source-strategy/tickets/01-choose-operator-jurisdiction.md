## Question

Which country should the recommendation treat as the home jurisdiction of the person or entity operating lofi.fm, and what assumptions should later research make if that operating location may change? Resolve this through a live grilling session so self-curated licensing and service eligibility are not evaluated against an unspecified legal context.

## Comments

### Resolution

Closed as out of scope. The operator is currently a UK-resident individual hobbyist, but this effort will not choose a durable operator jurisdiction or resolve jurisdiction-specific legal obligations. Later source research should expose unresolved rights and geographic constraints without treating them as legal conclusions; legal review can be revisited when implementation or commercialization makes it necessary.
