## Question

How do a shared continuously programmed Station and an independent per-listener Playlist differ for a public worldwide lofi.fm service? Compare listener behavior, music-rights and reporting categories, source-catalog requirements, compatibility with the current live-radio domain and player, managed-service availability, metadata, reliability, bandwidth and scaling, and hobby-scale cost. Create a linked research asset that makes the consequential trade-offs clear without selecting the model on the human's behalf.

## Comments

### Resolution - 2026-08-19

Compared the models across every requested dimension in [Self-Curated Playback Models](../research/self-curated-playback-models.md).

A shared continuously programmed Station preserves lofi.fm's communal join-live behavior, current Station vocabulary, and narrow direct-stream player contract, and it has mature managed AutoDJ/stream-hosting options. It can qualify for linear or noninteractive licensing routes only when the complete service meets territory-specific programming, metadata, source-copy, and reporting conditions; the architecture itself supplies no worldwide licence.

An independent per-listener Playlist provides separate start/resume positions and cheap, cacheable object delivery, but requires a new finite-track domain/runtime plus per-listener authorization and usage accounting. A separate cursor does not alone decide the legal category: track choice, personalization, foreknowledge, pause/resume, skip, seek, shuffle, caching, and territory-specific terms determine whether noninteractive, customized, interactive, on-demand, or direct-licence rules apply. Managed object storage is readily available, but no reviewed primary source established a hobby-priced turnkey service combining this custom playback model with worldwide music rights and reporting.

Neither model was selected. The linked research identifies the consequential product and risk choices for the human-owned [Choose the Self-Curated Playback Model](04-choose-self-curated-playback-model.md) ticket.
